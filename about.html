Sixty-two years in Southbridge

Boney's Barbershop was established in 1962 by Mr. Liston Boney. After an ownership change in 2007, the shop was renamed Boney's Barbershop, LLC. It has stayed family oriented and remains one of the longest lasting businesses in the city, offering some of the lowest prices in the area.

The people who cut here

Mr. Liston Boney (1941-2012) founded the shop. Mr. Virgil "Moose" Stewart (1950-2012) cut hair here for decades, including the first haircut of Carolyn Markland's son, who would be 48 today. Amere Robinson is the current owner and proprietor.

How the shop runs

Appointment only. The front door is locked between appointments, so please knock for service. A $15 missed-appointment fee applies. Masks are no longer required. Contactless payment is available. Tuesday through Saturday, beginning at 5am. Closed Sunday and Monday.
